What this shows

Every object in the public catalogue, propagated in your browser with SGP4 from the latest element sets, and the daily results of the ThermoSwarm Sentinel run:

  • Close approaches from an all-against-all screening of the catalogue over 72 hours. Each collision probability (Pc) uses an error model of public element sets calibrated on a year of data, and the storm-drag uncertainty measured that day by the ThermoSwarm sensor network. Pc max is the highest value inside the drag interval; diluted means the orbit is too uncertain for a low Pc to be reassuring.
  • Avoidance options: the smallest burn, and when, that brings Pc below 1 in a million.
  • Alerts: unannounced manoeuvres (storm-aware), break-ups, predicted re-entries with calibrated windows, drag-signature changes and track gaps.

Limits

Research prototype by an independent student. Not an operational conjunction service, and not affiliated with or endorsed by any agency. Element sets carry no covariance; the Pc values here are estimates, not conjunction data messages. Risk thresholds (high ≥ 1e-4, medium ≥ 1e-6) are illustrative.
